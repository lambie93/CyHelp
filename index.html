What is cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the practices that people use to protect computer systems and networks from cyber attacks.

These people can be governments/nations, individuals, companies, community organizations, and hackers.

The CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is the model used to discuss cybersecurity. CIA stands for confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
